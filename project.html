01. OVERVIEW

Why Study Saathi?

Students often manage attendance, notes, tasks, timetables, and study resources across multiple apps. Study Saathi brings the essential tools together into one focused experience, with the goal of reducing friction and helping students build consistent study habits.

“A small step towards helping students build a better tomorrow.”— Aditya Raj
